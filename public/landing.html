About Barangay Pembo

A proud barangay of Taguig City

Barangay Pembo is part of the Enlisted Men's Barrio (EMBO), a cluster of communities originally established to house military personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines. Its name honors the "Panthers" — the elite First Ranger Regiment.

Freshly welcomed as one of Taguig City's barangays, Pembo carries forward a tradition of service while embracing a connected, digital future through Pembo e-Hub.
